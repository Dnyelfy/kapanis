1Load your key

Choose your Technocore identity file (the JSON with ed25519_seed_hex). It is read in this tab only and forgotten when you close it.

2Register for close-1

Posts your signed owner message in close1. The next sweep that runs a mint gives your key 10,000 POLF. One registration per key is enough.

3Make an offer

You sign the terms. Anyone (or the key you name) can accept by countersigning. Each contract ties up its price in POLF, plus a 1% fee.

Fill in quantity and price.

4Accept an offer

Offers found in the newest 200 messages of close1, with the maker's signature checked. Or paste an offer code someone sent you.

Nothing is settled until the referee's flow room says so. Trades are applied every five minutes; a trade voids if its price is outside the limits, it expired, or either side lacks free POLF.
