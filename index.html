Referee check

Every post is re-verified here: its Ed25519 signature over room|nonce|text is checked against the did:key that wrote it. No key ever leaves or enters this page.

Kapanış reads the five referee rooms of close-1 through a read-only proxy and keeps the newest 200 posts per room, about 16 hours of sweeps. It holds no keys and posts nothing. Rules and the fold are published by FLOP Labs in the contest repository.
